What every lib in here gives you

  • Plugs into @angular/forms/signals via FormValueControl + [formField]
  • Schema-driven validation (required, readonly, disabled, validate, …) owned by the consumer's form()
  • Custom outlined styling — restyle via CSS custom properties without ::ng-deep
  • Standalone components, tree-shakable (sideEffects: false)
  • OnPush change detection, signal-based inputs end-to-end
